How it works
How it works

Three steps, and you're out of your room.

No profiles to maintain, no follower counts, nothing to keep up with. Post what you're doing, see who's in.

1

Broadcast

Free for two hours? Put it on the feed with a place, a time and a vibe. Cap the numbers if you want a small group.

2

Join

Tap into anything live on campus. Everyone who joins gets a group chat, so plans stop dying in a group DM.

3

Meet

Message someone you've never crossed paths with and you get one icebreaker until they reply. Quiet by design.

Nothing sticks around

Everything is gone in twenty-four hours.

Events expire when they end. The Recap holds the last day of campus, and then that goes too. There's no archive, no old posts, nothing following you into next term.

Built for a campus

Verified students only.

Sign-in is tied to your college Google account. Handles are claimed once and never change, so the person you met at an event is the same person in your inbox tomorrow.
